# UI/UX Pro Max: Production Craft & Anti-AI-Slop Architecture

A battle-tested design intelligence system created by senior product designers to prevent AI models from falling into statistical template traps (cliché cards, badge vomit, floating purple/cyan neon orbs, awkward secondary buttons).

---

## 1. The Core Philosophy: "Build an Instrument, Not a Website"

Every interface must feel like an intentional physical instrument crafted for a specific human in a specific situation:
- **Target Context:** Individuals with dysarthria, ALS, Stroke, or Cerebral Palsy.
- **Physical Reality:** Motor spasticity, tremors, low vocal stamina, handheld smartphones or wheelchair-mounted tablets.
- **Design Metaphor:** Precision audio studio gear (Teenage Engineering, Braun, Dieter Rams, Linear). Heavy, tactile, silent, dignified.

---

## 2. Hard Anti-Patterns (BANNED FOREVER)

1. **Jargon in User Interface:**
   - ❌ Never write "(TTS)", "(ASR)", "API Gateway", "Model Layer" on buttons or cards.
   - ✅ Human language only: "Speak", "Listen", "Quick Phrases".
2. **Button Strip Clutter:**
   - ❌ Never add a row of 3–4 competing action buttons (`Vocalize`, `Stop`, `Copy`) directly below hero content with muddy borders.
   - ✅ Auto-execute expected outcomes. Provide subtle icon micro-tools in corners only when strictly necessary.
3. **Badge & Pill Vomit:**
   - ❌ Never sprinkle 5+ pill badges across the screen with icons like `Sparkles`, `ShieldCheck`, `Activity`.
   - ✅ Single discrete hardware status indicator: `● Offline Edge ASR • 250ms`.
4. **Neon / Cyberpunk Glows:**
   - ❌ Radioactive teal dropshadows, blurred gradient orbs, cyan glow rings.
   - ✅ Pure deep obsidian (`#09090b`), matte charcoal cards (`#121214`), subtle 1px mechanical borders (`#222226`), and single-point analog amber/red tally LEDs.

---

## 3. Mobile Thumb-Zone Ergonomics & Motor Accessibility

On a smartphone:
1. **The Hero Stage (Top 50%):** Large, dignified typography card displaying the reconstructed words with zero distraction.
2. **The Waveform Bridge (Center):** Clean vertical soundwave amplitude bars giving immediate kinetic visual confirmation that phonation is occurring.
3. **The Mechanical Action Zone (Lower Thumb Zone):**
   - The primary record trigger is a weighted circular dial/push button located in the natural thumb sweep zone.
   - Touch target minimum: **56px $\times$ 56px** (ideally 112px–128px for master mic).
4. **Quick Assist Soundboard (Bottom Anchored):**
   - Immediate access to single-tap emergency cards (*Water*, *Caregiver*, *Yes*, *No*).
   - Touch targets are spaced and weighted to prevent accidental tremor mis-taps.
5. **Evaluator / Debug Tools (Decoupled at Bottom):**
   - Clinical benchmarks, judge evaluation matrices, and model comparison drawers belong strictly at the bottom or in a dedicated collapsible tab. A patient never has to look at them.

## 4. Visual Spec (Matching VoiceBridge Mockup)

* **Canvas:** `#09090b` (Pure Matte Obsidian)
* **Card Surface:** `#121214` with `border: 1px solid #222226` and `border-radius: 1.25rem` (20px)
* **Active Amber Tally Accent:** `#f59e0b` / `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.9)`
* **Typography:**
  - Display Text: High-contrast chalk `#f4f4f5`, fluid sizing `clamp(1.5rem, 4vw, 2.5rem)`
  - Telemetry: Monospace `#71717a`, uppercase tracking-widest
